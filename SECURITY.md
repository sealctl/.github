# Security

Please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's security advisories on the affected repository, for example [sealctl/sealctl](https://github.com/sealctl/sealctl/security/advisories/new). Don't open a public issue.

You get a first answer within three working days. Fixed issues are credited in the release notes unless you prefer otherwise.

## Safe harbour

We welcome good-faith security research. If you follow this policy, we will not pursue or support legal action against you, and we will treat your research as authorised. That means: test only with your own phone, words and machines; never access, change or keep other people's data; don't degrade the relay (no denial-of-service or load tests) and don't test Cloudflare's, Paddle's, Apple's or Google's systems; give us a reasonable time to fix before you publish (90 days by default, sooner by agreement). If a third party takes action against you for research done under this policy, we will say that it was authorised.
